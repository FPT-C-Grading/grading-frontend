010. University admission eligibility

Complex Logic
Write a C/C++ program to determine whether a student is eligible for university admission.

Inputs read in order:
  • age: Student's age (integer)
  • math: Mathematics score (float/double)
  • english: English score (float/double)
  • programming: Programming score (float/double)
Eligibility conditions (all must be met):
  • Age is between 18 and 25, inclusive.
  • Mathematics score ≥ 6.0
  • English score ≥ 5.0
  • Programming score ≥ 6.5
  • Average score of the three subjects ≥ 6.0
Display Eligible or Not Eligible.
Sample Input
20
7.0 6.0 8.0
Sample Output
Eligible
