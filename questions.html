Question 10: University admission eligibility

Write a C/C++ program to determine whether a student is eligible for university admission.
The program reads:

  • age: Student's age (integer)
  • math: Mathematics score (float)
  • english: English score (float)
  • programming: Programming score (float)
The student is eligible if all of the following conditions are satisfied:
  • The student's age is between 18 and 25, inclusive.
  • The Mathematics score is at least 6.0.
  • The English score is at least 5.0.
  • The Programming score is at least 6.5.
  • The average score of the three subjects is at least 6.0.
Otherwise, the student is not eligible. The program should display Eligible or Not Eligible. 


Input
20
7.0 6.0 8.0
Output
Eligible

Input
26
7.0 6.0 8.0
Output
Not Eligible
